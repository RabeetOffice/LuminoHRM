# Lumino HRM Phase 4 (Leave) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Staff apply for leave in the app, their manager and HR approve it, balances per employee, type and year are always right, approved leave lands in attendance as On Leave (full or half day), and everyone sees who is off on a calendar, the dashboard and the profile.

**Architecture:** Four layers kept apart. (1) **Records**: `leave_types`, `leave_balances` (allocated, adjustment, opening used; one row per employee, paid type and year), `leave_requests`, and `leave_days` (one row per counted day of an approved request: 1 or 0.5, half `full|am|pm`). (2) **Rules**: two pure-ish services: `LeaveDays` (which dates count: own shift's working days minus holidays, inside the employment window) and `LeaveBalances` (remaining = allocated + carried + adjustment − opening used − sum of leave days; carried is worked out when read, never stored). `LeaveRouting` decides who approves which step. (3) **Actions**: apply, decide, cancel, adjust and import, each in one transaction with row locks, re-checks and an audit entry, mails queued after commit. (4) **Attendance link**: the rebuilder reads `leave_days` for each employee-day it decides and passes full-day leave (`DayInput::onLeave`) or half-day leave (new `DayInput::leaveHalf`) to the calculator; approving or cancelling marks the affected days for rebuild. Payroll (Phase 5) reads `leave_days` and `attendances`.

**Tech Stack:** As Phases 0–3 (Laravel 13, PHP 8.4, Inertia + React + TypeScript, Tailwind 4, shadcn/ui, Wayfinder, spatie permission + activitylog, maatwebsite/excel, Pest, Vitest). No new UI, chart or calendar library: the calendar and the status chart are plain CSS grid / bars like the Phase 3 trend chart.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-phase-4-leave-design.md` (wins where it differs from the main design) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-lumino-hrm-design.md` sections 8 (roles), 9.1 (dashboard), 9.2 (profile tabs), 9.3 (attendance rule on half-day leave, line ~253), 9.4 Leave, 9.5 (unpaid leave deducted, opening figures), 9.10 Settings, 9.11 Audit, 11 (data model), 15 (defaults: Annual 14, Sick 8, Casual 10, Unpaid).

**Builds on:** Phase 3 as built (HEAD `4fbe1ac`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-24-phase-3-attendance.md`, ledger `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-24-phase-3-attendance/progress.md`, agent rules `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-25-phase-4-leave/agent-rules-phase3.md`). Holidays already exist (`App\Models\Holiday`, `HolidayController`, routes `holidays.*` under `/leave/holidays`, permission `settings.attendance-leave`); this phase reads them and does not rebuild them.

- **Balance formula (pin it everywhere):** `remaining(Y) = allocated + carried(Y) + adjustment − opening_used − used(Y)`, `used(Y)` = sum of `leave_days.amount` of that type dated in Y; `pending(Y)` = days of that type in Y of the employee's open requests; `available(Y) = remaining(Y) − pending(Y)`. `carried(Y)` = 0 for a type without carry-forward, else `min(max(0, remaining(Y−1)), carry_forward_cap)` (0 when there is no Y−1 row); when Y is after the current leave year (a projection), `remaining(Y−1)` is replaced by `remaining(Y−1) − pending(Y−1)`. ## Open decisions (defaults chosen; owner may change)

The design settles most behaviour. Where it is silent, or where the brief suggested otherwise, the plan uses these defaults, each marked "(default, owner may change)" where it is used:

1. **Year boundary:** a request may span 31 Dec → 1 Jan; each year's days are checked against that year's balance (design 5.3 — the design wins over the brief's "refuse").
2. **Past dates:** anyone may apply from 30 days back (`attendanceToday()` − 30) up to 31 December of next year; HR/Admin applying on behalf use the same window (design 5.4). Older history goes in as opening used or an adjustment.
3. **Pro-rating** (`leave.prorate_new_joiners`, default on): `quota × months from the joining month to December inclusive ÷ 12`, rounded down to the nearest 0.5; joining in January = full quota.
4. **Allocation job:** `hrm:leave-allocate`, scheduled hourly at :40; creates missing balance rows for the current leave year for everyone employed in it (idempotent, so a missed 1 January is caught up on the next run). Carry-forward needs no job: it is worked out when read.
5. **Carry-forward cap lives on each type** (Annual: carry forward, cap 5; others off); the Settings "carry-forward cap" input is removed (design 5.1).
6. **Changing a type's carry-forward flag or cap** changes the carried figure of the current and later years (carried is derived); the type dialog says so.
7. **Half-day mapping:** `am` = first half, `pm` = second half; on a half-leave day the worked half needs at least the shift's half-day hours; late and early minutes are not counted; worked ≥ threshold → `present`, else (fewer minutes, a single punch, or no punches once closed) → `absent` (half a day deducted). A single punch on a closed half-leave day is `absent` with `needs_attention`. Overtime is counted by the normal rule.
8. **Two halves on one date** (an `am` and a `pm` request both approved) = a full leave day in attendance.
9. **Counted days** = the employee's working days (weekly offs from the closed attendance row's `shift_rules` snapshot for that date when there is one, else their current shift, else the default shift) minus holidays; a request of only offs/holidays is refused.
10. **Overlaps** with the employee's open or approved requests on any counted day are refused; an `am` and a `pm` half on the same date may coexist.
11. **HR applying on behalf** may also give the final approval of that request (it is the employee's request, recorded as applied by HR; audited).
12. **Cancelling an approved request (HR/Admin only, reason required):** HR picks "cancel from" (default `attendanceToday()`, earliest the request's first date, never a day in a locked payroll month). Days from that date are removed and refunded; earlier days stay ("partly cancelled", `cancelled_from` set). Design 5.6 removes only days from today; this extends it so HR can fix "the person actually worked" (design 5.7).
13. **Employee leaves:** approved leave days after the last working day (or from `attendanceToday()` for a leaver without a last working day) are removed automatically; open requests with any counted day outside the window are cancelled automatically; audited.
14. **Holiday added on a leave day:** that leave day is removed and refunded, the employee is mailed (design 5.8); a request left with no days becomes `cancelled`. Removing a holiday adds nothing back; the delete confirmation lists the affected approved requests.
15. **Calendar visibility:** HR/Admin see everyone's approved leave; a Manager sees own team's; an Employee sees holidays and own leave. Birthdays (name and day only, never the year; active and probation staff) are shown to everyone, as the design lists them for staff too.
16. **Current-year leave that eats next year's projected carry** after next-year leave was approved is allowed; next year then shows overdrawn (red) for HR to sort out.
17. **Balance adjustments** (allocated, adjustment, opening used) need a reason (5–255 characters); a result below zero needs `confirm_overdrawn`.
18. **Balance editing rights:** `leave.approve-all`, never one's own balance unless Admin, and an Admin or HR person's balance only by an Admin (the Phase 3 `mayTouchAccount` rule).

## Review Focus

3. **Attendance interaction:** backdated approval over closed, manual and pre-start-date days; cancellation part-way through; half-day leave with punches (enough, too few, one, none, open day); a full leave day with punches; today's leave visible before the nightly close. Tests in Tasks 6, 7.

Tests in Tasks 1, 6.

## Shared interfaces

- **`App\Support\Leave\LeaveRouting`** (Task 1) is the only place that decides who approves, sees, cancels or administers. Every controller and action calls it; no screen re-implements a rule.
- **`App\Support\Leave\LeaveDays`** and **`App\Support\Leave\LeaveBalances`** (Task 2) are the only places that count days and compute balances. Screens, the apply check, the approval re-check, the dashboard and the profile all call them.
- **`App\Support\Attendance\RebuildMarks::forLeaveDays(int $employeeId, array $dates): int`** (Task 1) is how any leave change tells attendance to recompute days.
- **Attendance on leave days** (Task 7): the Phase 3 exempt rule is kept: an open exempt day without punches is null (nothing recorded yet), also with half-day leave; a lone machine punch on a half-leave day takes its role from the midpoint of the half worked; a manual correction keeps the day's leave (half judged on the worked half, full leave stays `on_leave`; an override wins and clears `leave_half`).
- **`App\Actions\Leave\LeaveDayRemoval::remove(LeaveRequest $locked, array $dates, string $why, ?User $by): array`** (Task 6) is the one way leave days leave the ledger after approval (HR cancel, holiday refund, exit trimming).
